# Reflection

This project demonstrated that AI effectiveness depends heavily on prompt quality.

Initially, vague prompts produced generic and unfocused outputs. After applying structured prompt engineering techniques such as role assignment, constraints, and output specification, the quality improved significantly.

One key insight is that AI does not “understand” intent—it responds to patterns and instructions. Without clear guidance, it fills gaps incorrectly.

This aligns with AI limitations discussed in course materials: poorly defined problems lead to incorrect or inefficient results.

The most valuable concept learned was iterative refinement. Prompt engineering is not a one-step process; it requires testing, evaluation, and adjustment.

This project strengthened my ability to:
- Think critically about instructions
- Communicate intent clearly
- Use AI as a tool rather than rely on it blindly
